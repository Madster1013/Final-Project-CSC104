Current Projects

These are all my current projects that I'm working on.

Project Nephyrus

The story follows our main protagonist Nephyrus: a 23 year old adult trying to keep a low profile and live a normal life with her adopted father Baaj. All that comes literally crashing down when an escaped child science experiment lands in the outskirts and poorly attempts to attack the father and daughter duo. Together, the three must navigate a cold and unforgiving world while uncovering horrifying revalations, true identities, and memories that Nephyrus would prefer to remain buried.

The Poltergeist Division

When one has a strong connection to death, it opens the doors to a whole other world. And that often leads to trouble in Victorian Era London. Serenity Locke is one of the many who works and communicates with the dead, which helps when most of her team is such. Recruited under the S.I.S under the shared mission to maintain the balance and publicity of the supernatural, Serenity's job is to protect a world unforgiving to women, and even more so to "witches" should she get caught.

Tomato Potato

Iris couldn't be more down in the dumps. College, work, and dorms breathing down her neck over a roomate, life felt like it was against her. That is, until she meets Nova, a star-child wanting to learn more about Earth. The two form an unlikely friendship turned romance that unite differing worlds with a love that transcends the stars.
